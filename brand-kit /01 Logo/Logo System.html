FOLDER 01 · THE LOGO SYSTEM

A mark, woven.

The Stitch Fit mark is a single interlocking emblem — two threads finding each other and holding. It is at once initial, knot, and weave. Use it generously, but never carelessly.

CONCEPT

Two threads — one warm, one stark — cross at the centre to suggest a stitch caught mid-pass. The form is geometric, modernist, but the proportions are tailored: this is a mark for a cloth merchant, not a tech brand.

CONSTRUCTION

Built on an eight-unit grid inside a perfect circle, the stroke weight is fixed and the angles are square. The wordmark sits below with a fine rule punctuating the form.
